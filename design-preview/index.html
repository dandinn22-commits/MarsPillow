    Which side should I sleep on?

    Back sleepers usually prefer the lower edge under the neck. Side sleepers usually prefer the higher edge, which fills the space between the shoulder and the head. Try both during your first few nights.

    How does the 60-night trial work?

    Sleep on your Mars Pillow for up to 60 nights. If it isn't right for you, contact us and we'll arrange a return. TODO return terms

    What does the warranty cover?

    The pillow comes with a 3-year warranty. TODO what's covered

    How do I wash it?

    Unzip the cover and machine wash it cold, then air dry. Don't wash the foam core. Air it out instead. TODO confirm care

    Does it smell when I open it?

    New memory foam can have a light smell for a day or two. Let it air out in a ventilated room.
